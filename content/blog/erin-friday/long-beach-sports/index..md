Leaking an email from californiafamily.org, forwarded by Erin Friday on January 23rd.

This email calls for californians to pack a hearing on February 6th in Long Beack. They specifically note that this event allows for public comment, and they want to send as many people to give public comment as possible, and be present for a press conference.
