## The three bills and documents

The email links to Google Docs and the zip contains three “summary sheet” Word documents used to coordinate testimony.

### 1. SB552 – Allowing Sex Segregation

- **What it does:** Defines “biological sex” and says designations by biological sex don’t constitute unlawful discrimination. Updates non-discrimination law to **allow sex segregation** in restrooms, locker rooms, sports, prisons, and mental health/treatment facilities.
- **Hearing:** Senate Judiciary Committee, **Wednesday Feb 11 at 1:00 pm**, NH State House Room 103.
- **Online:** Register support at [gc.nh.gov](https://gc.nh.gov/remotecommittee/senate.aspx) before **midnight Feb 11**. Email testimony to committee members (see attached summary sheet).
- **Sponsor:** Sen. Kevin Avard.  
- **Context:** Previous sex-segregation bills were vetoed; Gov. Ayotte has said she’ll support “the right bill.”

### 2. HB1376 – “Parents’ Rights to Not Trans Kids”

- **What it does:** Says child-rearing “consistent with the child’s biological sex” is **not** abuse or endangerment, and removes it as a factor in family-law matters (adoptions, agency licensing, best-interest determinations). In practice this would insulate parents who refuse gender-affirming care from abuse/neglect findings.
- **Hearing:** House Children and Family Law Committee, **Tuesday Feb 10 at 1:00 pm**, 1 Granite Place Concord, Room 230.
- **Online:** Submit testimony at [gc.nh.gov House remote testimony](https://gc.nh.gov/house/committees/remotetestimony/default.aspx) before **midnight Feb 10**.
- **Sponsor:** Rep. Lori Korzen.  
- **Context:** NH already has a “Parental Bills of Rights” (HB10) and has restricted certain gender-affirming care for minors; surrounding states have shield laws.

### 3. HB1356 – Statute of Limitations for Detransitioners

- **What it does:** Extends the time to sue for “violation of the prohibition on medical procedures intended to change a minor’s gender” from **2 years to 10 years** after the minor reaches the age of majority (i.e. more time for detransitioner malpractice suits).
- **Hearing:** House Judiciary Committee, **Wednesday Feb 11 at 2:30 pm**, 1 Granite Place Concord, Room 230.
- **Online:** Submit at [gc.nh.gov House remote testimony](https://gc.nh.gov/house/committees/remotetestimony/default.aspx) before **midnight Feb 11**.
- **Sponsor:** Rep. Bob Wherry.
